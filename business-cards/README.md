# Syd Hoeper square business cards

- Finished size: 2.5 × 2.5 inches
- Artwork size: 2.75 × 2.75 inches
- Bleed: 0.125 inches on every edge
- Color palette: cream `#FFF9F0`, ink `#1C1220`, and the website’s pastel rainbow colors
- Typeface: Fraunces for display copy and Avenir Next for supporting copy

The SVG files are the editable master artwork. Before sending them to a printer, confirm the printer’s preferred color profile and either embed or outline the fonts in a vector editor. Keep all essential text inside the existing safe area.

The back includes a high-resolution QR code that points to `https://sydhoeper.com`.

Open `preview.html` in a browser to review both sides together.

## Background-only artwork

`business-card-background-front.svg` and `business-card-background-back.svg` contain only the cream canvas and continuous pastel ribbon. They use a 612 × 612 artframe and are designed for a left-to-right flip: the ribbon’s position, color, width, and direction match at both side edges. Open `background-preview.html` to review the pair.
