The idea

After visiting Norway, I wanted to make language practice feel less like memorizing vocabulary and more like completing a familiar task. A recipe gave the lesson a natural order and a concrete outcome.

Inspiration

Learn through context

Norwegian words and instructions appeared as part of making breakfast instead of as isolated flash cards.

Structure

Use the recipe as the flow

The order of ingredients and actions created a simple beginning-to-end learning sequence.

Challenge

Learn Unity by building

I taught myself the basics of scenes, interactions, sequencing, and AR while making the project.

Result

A small project with a useful first.

I earned 100% on the assignment and finished with a working AR game—and, more importantly, a foundation in Unity that I could build on in later interactive projects.
