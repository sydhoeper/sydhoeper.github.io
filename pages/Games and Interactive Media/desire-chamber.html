The Desire Chamber

The Devil tarot card became a framework for exploring pleasure, compulsion, attachment, and agency.

Symbol

Loose chains

The figures appear bound, but their chains can be removed.

Question

Pleasure or compulsion?

When does desire support a life, and when does it control one?

Guardian

Face the shadow self

A devil figure guards honesty about appetite, sexuality, and control.

Core idea

The chamber does not tell visitors what they should want. It asks them to be honest about why they want it.

The build

I sculpted the tunnels at human scale in VR, surfaced them in Substance Painter, assembled the scene in Unity, and built a first-person route through it.

Result

A philosophical concept became a place I could walk through.

The final Unity scene gave the original proposal spatial rhythm, material detail, and a clear point of view. It remains one of the school projects I am most proud of.
