VR Flight of the Honeybee

A virtual-reality game about completing a honeybee’s working loop: forage for nectar, return to the hive, and pass it to other bees.

The game loop

I built the experience around one complete trip between the landscape and the hive. Each stage gives the player a different task while preserving one clear goal: bring nectar home.

  1. 01

    Fly

    Control the bee and navigate through the environment.

  2. 02

    Collect

    Visit flowers and gather nectar before returning home.

  3. 03

    Return

    Carry the collected nectar back into the hive.

  4. 04

    Exchange

    Shoot small nectar spheres toward the other bees to pass it along.

What I built

The assignment pushed me beyond a single interaction. I had to connect movement, collection, two environments, and a final exchange into one understandable VR sequence.

Movement

Bee-scale flight

The player directly controls the bee, making navigation the foundation of every other action.

Progress

Nectar collection

Flowers turn exploration into a clear task and give the return trip a purpose.

Completion

Hive exchange

The nectar-sphere interaction gives players a distinct final action inside the hive.

Result

A complete VR loop from flower to hive.

I earned 100% on the class project and finished with a playable experience that connected locomotion, collection, environment transitions, and object exchange.

Next steps

If I continued the project, I would focus on making the flight more comfortable, the goals easier to read, and the hive feel more alive.

Comfort

Tune flight controls

Add sensitivity options and test movement with more players to reduce VR discomfort.

Feedback

Clarify each action

Strengthen visual and audio feedback for collecting, carrying, and exchanging nectar.

World

Expand the hive

Add more bee behaviors and environmental responses so the exchange feels connected to a living system.

What I’d do differently

I would prototype locomotion and comfort settings before building the full environment, test the complete game loop earlier, and make the nectar exchange understandable without verbal explanation.
