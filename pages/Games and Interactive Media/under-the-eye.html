The brief

The Outernet Foundation brought the team a deliberately provocative idea: use its shared AR platform to stage a “punk rock” installation at an AR conference. The experience contrasted AR’s creative potential with questions about surveillance, data collection, and commercial influence.

Experience

Make it site-specific

The scene needed to feel anchored in the courtyard rather than like content floating on top of it.

Tone

Beautiful and unsettling

The visual world had to invite people in while supporting a critique of the systems behind AR.

Constraint

Four weeks to install

Creative development, technical integration, team production, and polish happened against one fixed event deadline.

The balancing act

Make a strange idea feel intentional, technically coherent, and ready for a live audience.

What I owned

I was the primary person building and managing the Unity scene. I translated the brief into a working environment, made artistic and interaction decisions, and kept contributions from the wider team moving toward one experience.

Unity

Own the complete scene

I greyboxed the environment, integrated systems and assets, increased fidelity, and maintained the build as it evolved.

Art direction

Make the world cohere

I made visual and experiential decisions so the objects, movement, sound, materials, and shader effects supported the same tone.

Production

Direct intern contributions

I managed three NYU interns as they developed finalized assets, materials, and shader graphs for the scene.

How I built it

I started with spatial relationships and flow, then increased fidelity only after the scene worked as a whole. Daily check-ins with the lead artist and engineer kept the creative and technical work aligned.

  1. Greybox the scene
  2. Validate scale and flow
  3. Build and source assets
  4. Add materials, shaders, and sound
  5. Integrate and polish
Create

Mix real and placeholder assets

I used Blender and Meshy AI to create or rapidly prototype assets while the final visual language took shape.

Coordinate

Check in every day

The artist, engineer, and I reviewed progress daily so visual changes and technical implementation stayed connected.

Integrate

Protect the full experience

I reviewed incoming work in context and filled the gaps needed to make the complete installation feel deliberate.

Result

The team delivered the installation on schedule. It ran successfully at AWE, and the client publicly praised both the demo and the team that brought it to life.
