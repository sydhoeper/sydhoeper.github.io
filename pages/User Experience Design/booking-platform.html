Campendium · 2022

Campground Booking

I led Campendium’s first native reservation experience, connecting campground discovery to payment without losing the context RV travelers needed.

Overview

Campendium helped travelers choose a campground—but booking happened elsewhere.

Context

Research ended at a third-party handoff.

Objective

Launch native booking in two months.

Challenge

Account for equipment, capacity, rules, and price.

My role

I led the entire design—from service strategy through responsive UI, prototyping, and engineering handoff.

How I approached it

Preserve context

Third-party booking broke continuity. I designed one service from discovery through confirmation.

Design for fit

Availability was not enough. I prioritized the details that determined campsite compatibility.

Reduce delivery risk

I modeled the service early and partnered with product and engineering throughout delivery.

Process

The design evolved through four connected decisions, each reducing a different kind of uncertainty.

Frame the service

Find where trust broke

I traced the third-party handoff, then mapped the decisions and data Campendium would need to own.

Direction: Preserve campground context from discovery through confirmation.

Choose the entry point

Add booking without replacing research

I tested how prominently booking should appear, then extended Campendium’s familiar map-and-list results.

Decision: Signal bookability in search, then begin availability after a campground is chosen.

Find an available campsite

Show which campsites are available for their dates

Travelers wanted to see which specific campsites they could book for their trip without leaving Campendium.

Decision: Bring date-based, campsite-level availability into Campendium, then show essential fit details before reservation.

Resolve the transaction

Keep the booking reviewable

A stepped flow separated details customers needed together, so I consolidated the reservation into one page.

Delivery: Carry the same hierarchy into confirmation, responsive states, validation, and handoff.

Proposed solution

A single service connected bookable search, campground availability, campsite selection, checkout, and confirmation.

The experience shipped. Supply limited growth.

Launched in May 2022 after two months of design.

Delivered

One continuous service

Search through confirmation stayed in Campendium.

Enabled

Buildable handoff

Responsive, error, and email states were documented.

Revealed

Seven properties

Limited inventory prevented growth; the marketplace was later sunset.

What I carry forward

Validate supply, operations, and demand alongside usability before scaling a marketplace.
