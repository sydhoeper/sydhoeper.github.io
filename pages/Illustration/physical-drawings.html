Ongoing studio practice

Physical Drawings

Marker drawings, plein air sketches, and gouache paintings collected by medium in one evolving body of hand-made work.

Marker doodles

An evolving sketchbook of playful characters and visual experiments developed with layered Copic alcohol markers and detailed Micron fineliners since 2023.

Plein air sketches

A new practice of drawing directly from the places and moments around me, started in summer 2026.

Gouache paintings

Small paintings that explore bold color, symbolic imagery, and the tactile texture of opaque paint on paper.
