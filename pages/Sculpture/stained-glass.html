Ongoing practice · January 2025–present

Stained Glass

I began working in stained glass in January 2025. Every piece starts with an original design that I develop into a finished glass object.

Original designs I create every pattern myself rather than working from premade templates.
Learning through making Each finished piece helps me refine how an idea translates into shape, color, and light.
Featured piece · Commissioned by Peter Burr

ARIA, translated into glass

Commissioned by Peter Burr, this panel translates the pink-and-gray pipe composition from a screenshot of ARIA Engine into stained glass. I rebuilt the image as a cuttable pattern, bringing my digital worldbuilding and stained-glass practice into the same object.
