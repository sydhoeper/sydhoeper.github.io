The World of Emora Diorama

A mixed-media ecosystem where imagined plants are the inhabitants.

Slide 1 of 6

Concept and build

For the Worldbuilding class’s “Inhabitants” prompt, I made the landscape itself the cast—building a physical world around strange, character-like plant species.

Concept

Plants as characters

Recognizable silhouettes and creature-like details make each species feel alive.

Composition

One connected habitat

A lavender river links repeated species around the many-eyed anchor tree.

Making

Story through materials

Layered terrain, saturated color, texture, and scale make the ecosystem tactile.

Result

A world revealed through what grows there.

The finished diorama became a tactile ecosystem and the visual foundation for the wider World of Emora.
