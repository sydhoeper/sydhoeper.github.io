The assignment

“Inhabitants” asked us to decide what lives in our invented worlds. I chose not to begin with people or animals. Instead, I focused on the plant life that would define how Emora looks, feels, and functions.

Starting point

Plants as characters

Each species needed enough personality and visual identity to feel like an inhabitant rather than background decoration.

World logic

An ecosystem, not a lineup

I grouped forms around shared terrain and water so the inhabitants felt connected to one place.

Medium

Build the world physically

A diorama let color, texture, scale, and placement carry parts of the story that words could not.

Creative direction

The landscape was not a container for the inhabitants. The landscape was the cast.

Building a living system

I used a few recurring visual rules to make many strange forms feel related: recognizable plant silhouettes, unexpected creature-like details, repeated species, and one terrain system tying everything together.

Focal inhabitant

Blur plant and creature

The many-eyed tree makes the world feel observant and alive while remaining rooted in a plant form.

Species

Design families of forms

Repeated branching, hanging, feathered, and mushroom-like shapes suggest populations rather than one-off props.

Habitat

Let the river connect them

The winding lavender river creates a visual path through the piece and gives the plant clusters a shared environment.

From world to diorama

I organized the physical piece from large environmental decisions to small organism details, using composition and material contrast to keep a dense world readable.

  1. Shape the terrain
  2. Establish the river
  3. Place the anchor organism
  4. Build species families
  5. Layer color and texture

Result

A world revealed through what grows there.

The final diorama turned the assignment into the project I had been hoping to make: a dense, tactile ecosystem where plants carry character, narrative, and a sense of place. It also established a visual foundation I could continue exploring across the wider World of Emora.
