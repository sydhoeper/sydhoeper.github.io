Giant Paper Clips

Interlinked paper clips recreated at ten times their original scale.

 +

Make things big

I recreated a pair of interlinked paper clips at ten times their original scale using steel rod and a metal bender. To match the proportions, I made custom dies from aluminum blocks on a metal lathe, formed the first clip, threaded the second rod through it, and finished the ends with an angle grinder.

I suspended the forms with fishing line, applied Plasti Dip, masked each section for color, and finished the piece with a matte coat.

Giant Clothespin

An everyday wooden clothespin rebuilt at person-sized scale in corrugated cardboard.

 +

Make things big

The assignment was simple: make an ordinary object big. I translated a wooden clothespin into a person-sized model, building the two halves from corrugated cardboard and wrapping them in neon green and pink. Cutouts, angled ends, and a bent metal rod preserve the recognizable jaw, grip, and spring details at a radically different scale.

Team product design · 3 images

Playground Equipment

A full-scale, stand-on maze developed with a six-person team for Landscape Structures.

 +

From research to play

Landscape Structures sponsored our Toy Product Design course and asked teams to create a new playground “event.” Our six-person team designed a circular, stand-on maze supported by a large spring, with a marble that players guide by shifting their weight.

We used market research, playground visits, interviews with children, sketch models, rapid prototypes, peer reviews, pitches, and iteration to reach the final concept. The full-scale prototype combined CNC-routed high-density polyethylene, acrylic, steel hardware, and 3D-printed interior figures.

Cricket Condo

A modular cricket-farming system designed to make alternative protein production more efficient.

 +

Second-place concept

During the 10,000 Makes Marathon, teams of five had 24 hours to respond to the theme “Grand Challenges.” Our team focused on the land, water, and feed demands of conventional meat production and proposed crickets as a lower-resource protein source.

We designed a stackable farming system with screened walls for airflow, perforated floors, removable waste drawers, front access for food and water, and climate control for each shelving unit. I was the primary designer of the condo itself and built the prototype with one teammate in six hours.

We presented alongside eleven other teams and received second place.

Model making · 2 images

Tap Handle

A legible, modular tap handle designed for the circuit-inspired Tin Whiskers microbrewery.

 +

Built for change

Tin Whiskers wanted a tap handle that remained recognizable when rotated and could change color quickly for different beers. I developed a simple, ergonomic aluminum body with branding visible from multiple angles.

I cut the body from aluminum bar, CNC-engraved the lettering, spray painted and disc sanded the surface, and threaded the top and bottom. The top references a Wheatstone bridge circuit and was water-jet cut, finished with an angle grinder, and paired with interchangeable laser-cut acrylic and magnetic vinyl color pieces.
