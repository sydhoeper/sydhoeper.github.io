The Missoula Monster Project

My needle-felted interpretation of a kindergartner’s drawing for the 2024 Missoula Monster Project.

One monster, two imaginations

Edie’s monster eats rabbits and corn, lives in a house, and likes being in a meadow.

I recreated the blue, orange, and green character as a shadowbox using needle felt, wire, and miniature objects.

About the project

The Zootown Arts Community Center organizes the annual collaboration with the Missoula County Public Schools Fine Arts Department. The original drawings and artist interpretations are exhibited side by side at the ZACC Main Gallery, raising money for arts education and classroom supplies.
