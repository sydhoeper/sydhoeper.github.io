The Missoula Monster Project

A kindergartner imagined a monster. I translated it into a needle-felted world while keeping the original creature at the center.

Starting point A kindergartner’s original monster drawing
My medium Needle felting and mixed-media shadowbox
Exhibition Original and reinterpretation displayed side by side

One monster, two imaginations

The Missoula Monster Project pairs children’s drawings with adult artists who recreate the creatures in their own medium.

Edie imagined a monster that likes to eat rabbits and corn, lives in a house, and loves spending time in a meadow. For my interpretation, I turned the blue, orange, and green creature into a fuzzy shadowbox scene filled with those details.

Made for the community

Zootown Arts Community Center produces the annual project with the Missoula County Public Schools Fine Arts Department. The original drawings and finished artworks are exhibited together at the ZACC Main Gallery.

The family-friendly event celebrates the young artists and raises funds for arts education and needed classroom supplies.
