Approach

Research deeply. Make the system visible. Build toward clarity.

My process usually begins by mapping what is known, what is assumed, and where people are getting stuck. From there, I use research, diagrams, prototypes, and conversation to help teams build a shared understanding of the problem.

I care about accessibility, plain language, thoughtful interaction design, and making sure the people affected by a product are represented in the decisions behind it.

Education

Game Design & Interactive Media

I’m currently pursuing an MFA in Game Design and Interactive Media at the University of Montana, where I’m expanding my practice through augmented reality, virtual reality, creative coding, 3D environments, worldbuilding, and experimental interaction design.

My graduate work gives me space to explore ideas that don’t fit neatly inside conventional product design: desire, surveillance, utopia, community, embodiment, and the emotional possibilities of interactive spaces.
